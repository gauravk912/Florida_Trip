Day by day

The loop runs Keys first (Thursday traffic is lighter heading south than the weekend), then the Everglades, up the Gulf coast, to the springs, back across Alligator Alley to Miami Beach, and a Miami morning before the flight. Every day starts early. Days 3 and 4 are the long ones.

Weather and daylight

Late November is dry season, warm in the Keys and cooler as you go north. Check the forecast two days out.

81° / 72°F

Typical November high and low in Key West. Mostly sunny with about 1 stormy day in the month.

~79°F

Average Key West sea temperature in November. Warm enough to swim at Fort Zach and Bahia Honda.

~5:35 pm

Sunset all trip. The Keys hit their earliest sunset of the year on Nov 29 (5:32 pm). Sunrise is around 6:50 am.

Breezy

November is one of the windier months in Key West (about 12 mph). Mornings around Tampa and Crystal River can feel cool: pack a light jacket.
